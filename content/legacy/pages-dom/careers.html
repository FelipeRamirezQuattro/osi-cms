ORGANIZATION SUMMARY

 

Odessa Separator Inc (OSI) is a 25-year-old, locally-owned private company offering innovative downhole solutions for all the ALS to mitigate sand, gas and chemical issues downhole. OSI is focused on decreasing failures and increasing run time and well performance. The Company operates in over 5 countries and has 5 offices in the USA (North Dakota/Montana, Hobbs, Floresville, Odessa, Oklahoma,). OSI is a great place to build a career, and it provides opportunity for growth and upward mobility.

 

We pride ourselves in providing excellent training and resources to allow you to grow and build your skillset. Odessa Separator Inc. offers competitive compensation, medical, dental, vision, and matching 401K program benefits. We also offer paid vacation, company paid short-term disability, life insurance and continued education assistance. If you would like to be a part of a growing innovative team, OSI is the company for you!
